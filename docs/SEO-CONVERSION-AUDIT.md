# RBE Capital Equip. — search, conversion, technical SEO and performance audit

October 2026. This audit covers only the areas that were in scope: search research, search intent, AEO, GEO / entity clarity, conversion and contact details, technical SEO, performance and factual accuracy. The design, the 8-page architecture and the 3D backhoe experience were not changed.

---

## 1. Search research

### Method and limits

I ran live web searches (1 Oct 2026) for the brief's seed queries and their variants, then read the result pages and the question patterns they surfaced. The search tool's results are US-hosted and do not show local Indian Google SERPs, Google Ads volumes or People Also Ask boxes directly. **No search volume or keyword difficulty is claimed anywhere in this document or on the site.** Several competitor and government sites (desimachines.com, transrentals.in, kumaon.gov.in, nainital.nic.in, Wikipedia, developers.google.com) were blocked by this environment's network policy, so their content was checked through search-result extracts only.

### What the results showed

| Query family | Who ranks | What the pages do |
|---|---|---|
| `JCB rental Haldwani`, `JCB on hire Rudrapur`, `JCB on rent Nainital` | Justdial listings (including locality pages such as Motahaldu and Kusumkhera), Sulekha, IndiaMART supplier pages, national aggregators (transrentals.in, heavyequipments.in, rentkart), and one manufacturer dealer listing | Directory format: "JCB on rent", "best price", "near me", per-hour or per-month price, years in business, phone. Nobody explains terrain, mobilisation or machine fit |
| `JCB rental Almora / Bageshwar / Pithoragarh` | **No relevant rental providers.** Results were taxi and car-hire pages, Wikipedia and directories for other cities | The inner-hill districts are effectively unserved in organic results, which is an opening for a page that covers them accurately |
| `backhoe loader rental Uttarakhand` | National aggregators (Dehradun/Haridwar focus) and US rental chains | Nothing Kumaon-specific |
| `backhoe loader for hill road construction`, `backhoe vs excavator` | Manufacturer and blog content (SANY, Equipment World, equipmentsdekho, dozr, US dealers) | Informational: what the machine does, dig depth, mobility on wheels compared with an excavator's digging power |
| `JCB rent per hour` / `monthly`, `JCB 3DX rent` | IndiaMART, TradeIndia, rate-card blogs | Price is quoted per hour (diesel included or excluded), per 8-hour day, or per month with a cap on hours (e.g. "240 hours, then per-hour"). Common terms: **dry hire** (machine only) and **wet hire** (with operator and usually diesel) |
| Contractor-type questions (`questions to ask before hiring JCB`, long-term rental) | Blogs and guides | Recurring buyer questions: what is included (operator, diesel, transport), duration and discounts, site access, digging depth, soil, breakdowns |
| Kumaon monsoon and road news | News and government (newsonair.gov.in) | Landslides close hill roads every monsoon, and JCBs are stationed at slide zones. This supports the site's monsoon planning advice |

### Real search patterns used (not invented)

- **Commercial / local:** "JCB rental [town]", "JCB on hire [town]", "JCB on rent [town]", "earthmovers on rent [town]", "building machinery rental [town]", "backhoe loader rental [state]"
- **Near me:** Justdial titles build "near me" into local listings, so local intent is served by directories. RBE answers it through entity/location clarity rather than "near me" pages.
- **Price / duration:** "JCB rent per hour", "monthly rent", "per day", diesel included or excluded, dry hire or wet hire
- **Suitability:** "backhoe loader for road construction / hill road", "backhoe vs excavator", "how deep can a JCB dig", "is JCB a backhoe loader"
- **Contractor planning:** what information a quote needs, what is included, site access, long-term contracts, breakdowns
- **Brand-as-generic:** "JCB" is used as the generic word for a backhoe loader, even for 3DX-class model queries

### Terminology decision (JCB vs backhoe loader)

People search "JCB", but JCB is a manufacturer's trademark and RBE has not confirmed that its machines are JCB-made. The site therefore:

- uses **"backhoe loader"** as the product term everywhere;
- mentions "JCB" only as a search synonym ("often called a JCB in India, after one manufacturer") in a few meta descriptions, the glossary and the FAQ "Is a JCB the same as a backhoe loader?";
- states that the make and model of the machine offered is given in the quote. It never implies a JCB-branded fleet.

## 8. Performance — measured

### How it was measured

- Production build (`astro build`), served locally with gzip and immutable caching on hashed assets and fonts, which approximates a static host.
- **Lighthouse 12** with headless Chromium at mobile settings (Moto G-class, 4× CPU slowdown, slow 4G) and desktop settings. Two throttling methods:
  - *Simulated* (Lighthouse default) on all 8 pages, mobile and desktop, before and after.
  - *Applied DevTools throttling* (a real throttled load), 3 runs each, on the home page.
- Functional browser tests with Playwright.
- **Important limit:** this container has **no GPU**. WebGL runs in SwiftShader (software rendering on the CPU), so every frame of the 3D scene counts as main-thread CPU time. **TBT and long-task numbers for the home page are therefore much worse than on a real phone or laptop with a GPU.** They are useful for comparing before and after, not as absolute values. Real-user INP has to come from field data.

### Reading the numbers honestly

- **Meaningful content loads in under 2 seconds on every page on mobile:** the hero H1 and copy paint at about 1.35 s under real 4× CPU and slow-4G throttling, and inner pages reach LCP at 1.65–1.83 s even in simulated mode. Desktop is 0.36–0.46 s.
- The simulated home-page LCP (2.57 s, down from 3.25 s) is a *projection* that adds the CPU cost of software WebGL to the critical path. The observed throttled paint is about 1.35 s. On a GPU device the projection would be lower; this cannot be proven here.
- **TBT / INP on the home page was not proven to improve.** The longest single task is about 30% shorter, but total blocking time in this GPU-less container is dominated by WebGL frames, and run-to-run noise is large. It must be checked with real-device or field data (Search Console Core Web Vitals / CrUX) after launch. **No under-200 ms INP claim is made.**
- CLS on the home page is 0.011–0.012 after the change, versus 0.000–0.003 before. That is still well inside "good" (≤ 0.1). The source was not isolated in these runs.

### What was changed (and why only this)

| Change | Reason |
|---|---|
| Preload `instrument-serif-italic.woff2` (22 KB) | Every H1 has a serif-italic phrase. When this font swapped in late, the H1 re-wrapped and moved the content below it. This caused the 0.100 and 0.128 CLS |
| 3D scene built in chunks (`scheduler.yield()` / `setTimeout` between environment, ridges, ground, trees, props and machine) | Construction used to run as one blocking task. The visuals are the same |
| `renderer.compileAsync()` before the first frame | Uses `KHR_parallel_shader_compile` where the GPU driver supports it, so the first frame does not stall while shaders compile |
| A failed WebGL init now falls back to the static layout | Before, a context failure left the hero in a broken half-state |
| SVG map coordinates rounded to 0.1 | Smaller HTML on the map pages |

Already in place and kept unchanged: Three.js loads lazily after `load` + idle in a separate chunk (591 KB minified, 149 KB gzip), with the static poster and copy visible before it loads. The scene renders only while the hero is on screen and the tab is visible. Mobile uses a lower pixel ratio, smaller shadow map and fewer trees. Reduced-motion and no-WebGL users get the static layout. JavaScript on first load is about 2 KB gzip (3 KB on the contact page). Home HTML is 21 KB gzip with critical CSS inlined. Fonts: 5 self-hosted Latin-subset WOFF2 files (111 KB in total), 3 of them preloaded.

**Not done, on purpose:** dropping shadows, antialiasing, trees or the intro animation, or delaying the scene until the first scroll. Each would trim lab TBT but would visibly change the approved 3D experience. That decision belongs to the owner, ideally after real-device data.

## 10. Remaining dependencies (external only)

1. **Production domain.** Set: `https://rbecapitalgroup.in`. Canonical, Open Graph, sitemap, robots.txt and schema URLs use it by default.
2. **Host redirects** on the chosen host: HTTP→HTTPS, www → apex (a Cloudflare Redirect Rule), and non-trailing-slash → trailing-slash 301s, configured on the host. They cannot be verified from the repository.
3. **Field data:** submit the sitemap in Search Console once the site is live, and check real-user Core Web Vitals (CrUX/Search Console) after traffic builds up. The lab numbers above come from a container with no GPU.
4. **Optional, only if the business confirms:** a Rotoblast Engineering website URL (`site.parent.url`), a form/CRM endpoint (`site.formEndpoint`), an operator policy, service commitments or machine models. Each can then be stated. None is currently claimed.
